## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (no build step), same model as the current site. Repo: github.com/Chibo-dev/Daphine-Santana, domain daphinesantana.com.br. Two pages: home (`/`) and link-in-bio (`/links/`).

## Users

Primary: mothers and fathers in Mogi das Cruzes and the Alto Tietê region who noticed something in their child's development (delayed sitting/walking/speech, coordination, sensory sensitivity, attention, suspected TEA/TDAH, school difficulties) and are looking for orientation, often arriving from Instagram or Google on a phone, worried and unsure where to start.

Secondary: families seeking gerontopsicomotricidade for an elderly relative; adults/PCD/autistic/injured people seeking treino físico adaptado.

## Product Purpose

Website for Espaço Daphine Santana. It must turn a worried visit into a WhatsApp conversation with Daphine to book an initial evaluation. Success = WhatsApp taps from qualified families in the region.

## Positioning

Daphine personally evaluates and follows each child with an individual plan, either at her clinic in Mogi das Cruzes or at the family's home anywhere in the Alto Tietê. Psicomotricidade aquática is rare in the region. Seven specialties sit under one integrated care plan.

## Product Principles

1. The parent's worry comes first: recognize it, then offer a calm next step.
2. Every path ends in one action: talk to Daphine on WhatsApp.
3. Show the real person and the real space; trust comes from Daphine herself.
4. Local and concrete: Mogi das Cruzes and named Alto Tietê cities, clinic or home.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience includes families of children with disabilities and PCD adults: readable type sizes, strong contrast, large tap targets, reduced-motion support, mobile-first.
